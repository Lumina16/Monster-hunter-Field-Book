# Completed features

## Libraries

The frozen catalogue includes 3,544 weapons, 1,901 items, 403 crafting/melding records, 71 large monsters and 522 quests. Equipment coverage includes armour and armour sets, decorations, skill levels and slot requirements, charms, Kinsects, Palico equipment/gadgets, specialised tools, canteen ingredients and food skills. Small monsters and endemic life have dedicated catalogue records.

Weapons have Guide, Database and Family Tree views. Family nodes use rarity-coloured icons and applicable element/status symbols. The Info control opens the selected weapon's database entry. Forge/Upgrade choices show applicable methods. Weapon-photo overlays and experimental movement GIFs were removed at the user's request.

Resources and Recipe are combined under Item Libraries. Items link to gathering, drops, quests and uses through expandable groups. Empty recipe-details controls are omitted. Search fields use concise placeholders and bounded clear controls.

## Maps and reference

Layered locale maps, world/base atlases and facility directories are available. Locale cards show smaller static monster images with tappable profile links. Field notes provide searchable topics, tips and mechanics; entire cards open the note. Credits are expandable and reference links carry descriptive names.

## Story routes

Speedrun presents required story progression as staged checklists. Completing a stage reveals the next stage with a connecting line/circle animation. Checkboxes remain reversible. Iceborne unlocks after World completion, with a Continue transition and themed effects. Route resets require confirmation.

## Presentation and preferences

- World/Iceborne themes, custom colour wheels and multiple theme presets.
- Gradients, saved visual presets, font size, line/letter/word spacing and icon size.
- Compact/comfortable density and exact manual Bestiary columns with rows per batch.
- Animation enable/speed controls, transition styles and previews.
- Original gold touch ripple, alternative touch effects, ambient controls and light performance mode.
- Circular overview search trigger, theme-aware pulses and enlarged overview logo.
- Smooth expandable groups and panel transitions, with reduced-motion support.
- Startup destination, sorting, remembered reading state and section position.
- Bookmarks, music preferences, backup/import review, selective resets and Reset All Settings.

Latest refinement: every Settings group starts closed; Gradients and Saved visual presets have the same gap as peer boxes. Reading-group persistence elsewhere is retained.
